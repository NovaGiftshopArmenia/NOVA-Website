## Key Findings

### What is GEO?

**Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** is a framework to help content creators improve their visibility in **generative engine responses** (AI search engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews).

Unlike traditional SEO (ranking in search results), GEO focuses on **being cited** by AI systems.

### Core Results

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Maximum Visibility Boost** | Up to 40% |
| **Low-ranking Sites Boost** | Up to 115% with citations |
| **GEO-bench Dataset** | 10,000+ queries across domains |
| **Tested on** | Perplexity.ai (commercial GE) |

### Why GEO Matters

1. Traditional search engines are being replaced by generative engines
2. Content creators have little control over how their content appears in AI responses
3. GEO provides systematic methods to improve visibility
4. Different domains need different strategies

## The 9 Optimization Methods

### Method 1: Cite Sources (+40%)
Add authoritative citations and references to your content.
AI systems prefer content that appears well-researched. Citations signal authority.

Example:
BEFORE: "Studies show that fragrance longevity depends on concentration."
AFTER: "According to the International Fragrance Association (IFRA), Extrait de Parfum contains 20-40% aromatic compounds, producing 8-12 hours of longevity versus 2-4 hours for an Eau de Cologne."

---

### Method 2: Statistics Addition (+37%)
Include specific numbers, data points, and quantitative information.
Numbers make content more quotable and verifiable.

Example:
BEFORE: "Many people buy perfumes online in Armenia."
AFTER: "Online fragrance sales in Armenia grew by 34% between 2023 and 2025, with gift set purchases peaking during the holiday season."

---

### Method 3: Quotation Addition (+30%)
Add expert quotes with proper attribution.
Quotes from recognized experts boost credibility.

Example:
BEFORE: "Francis Kurkdjian is a celebrated perfumer."
AFTER: "Francis Kurkdjian, the Armenian-French master perfumer behind Baccarat Rouge 540, has described his creative philosophy as 'translating emotion into scent — the most intimate form of self-expression.'"

---

### Method 4: Authoritative Tone (+25%)
Write with confidence and expertise. Avoid hedging language.

AVOID: "This might help...", "I think...", "possibly..."
USE: "This strategy improves...", "The data confirms...", "NOVA's curation ensures..."

---

### Method 5: Easy-to-Understand (+20%)
Simplify complex concepts for broader accessibility.
AI aims to provide helpful answers to users of all knowledge levels.

NOVA Application: Always define perfumery jargon immediately after using it.
Example: "Sillage — the invisible trail a fragrance leaves in the air as you move through a room."

---

### Method 6: Technical Terms (+18%)
Include domain-specific terminology.
Signals expertise to AI systems.

NOVA Application: Use precise terms: Aromatic Fougere, Extrait de Parfum, Absolute Incense, calone, maceration, olfactive pyramid, chypre, coumarin.

---

### Method 7: Unique Words (+15%)
Increase vocabulary diversity. Avoid repetitive phrasing.

---

### Method 8: Fluency Optimization (+15-30%)
Improve readability and flow. Well-structured, grammatically clean content scores higher.

---

### Method 9: AVOID Keyword Stuffing (-10%)
Keyword stuffing actively **hurts** GEO visibility. Write naturally.
